What if I can't afford £500?

I charge for coaching because I can't help everyone individually, but I do my best. Everything I think and teach is available for free across all my content, and you can join the community for direct messaging with me at no cost. What makes this different from therapy?

I'm not a therapist, this is mentorship. Think of it more like a training partner for your life: weekly calls, stuff to work on between them, and someone checking in mid-week who actually gets what you're going through because he's been there.

What if I don't follow through?

That's what the weekly calls and mid-week check-ins are for. I'll notice before you do. The guarantee runs on the same logic: it protects you if you do the work and it doesn't pay off, but it only holds up if you actually show up. If you're not willing to try, don't apply.

Is this just self-improvement stuff?

No. Most self-improvement starts from the idea that you're broken and need fixing, which is just the voice in your head winning. This is about understanding yourself. The changes come after that, almost on their own.
